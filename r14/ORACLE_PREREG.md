# Offline discovery-value audit preregistration

No new online policy and no official calls. The oracle may use the recovered local hidden world ONLY to label downstream costs; no oracle labels, true N, or scene truth enter a deployable planner. Full posterior-world rollout remains deferred until candidate recall is known.

Sample a fresh baseline pool of1024 scenes:512 with N=16,512 cycling N=10..15, selected by N only. Use a fixed hash namespace, no performance filtering. Collect all actual R12 discovery decision snapshots. Select unique states with fixed RNG:500 early,500 middle,500 late (phase by thirds of a scene's baseline discovery decision sequence), plus500 additional N16-late states excluded from the previous selections. If unique support is insufficient, stop selection and enlarge the pool; never duplicate states. Report these enriched strata separately, not as population-unweighted estimates.

For each selected state, evaluate ALL remaining nodes. First perform the R12 first-node continuation and require exact saved remaining cost. Every candidate executes its full real sweep (or legal early termination), then frozen R12 to completion in a cloned Engine and cloned solver/particle RNG state. Use positive regret relative to the best among these candidate continuations; this is Q_R12 under a known world, not globally optimal Q*. Save branch action logs, cost, stage components, H1(new channel within first sweep), H2(any such source cleared within120 virtual seconds of its first observation), H3(first sweep reaches16 confirmed).

Prediction ranking is probability_any with stable R12-order ties; additionally retain expected_new. Compare Spearman (undefined ties reported, not filled with0), top1 best-set inclusion, top3 candidate recall, top3 overlap and regret of predictor/R12. Report ties explicitly. Cluster intervals by scene; no treating2000 states as2000 independent scenes. N strata, phase and probability margin are descriptive, not posthoc policy adoption gates.

Residual R12 time, remaining planned sweep cost and hypothetical release-cost proxies are descriptive weights, not proven marginal benefits. A small residual time or early hit does not establish low value. Actual one-step paired continuation is the stronger causal intervention within this simulator. H3 is structurally impossible for N<16. Numerical10-30s hit values in the proposal are hypotheses, not measured facts.
